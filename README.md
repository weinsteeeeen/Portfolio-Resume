# Portfolio-Resume
Electrical Engineering student at UNSW seeking an internship to apply skills in circuit design, embedded systems, electronics, power systems, automation, and practical engineering projects.

**E-Communication Device for Remote Bushfire Areas (2026) (Uni Project)**
Designed and developed a weatherproof, solar-powered communication device for remote areas without grid power or internet access. Integrated long-range radio communication, environmental sensing, a display interface, and solar power to provide automated updates on trail conditions, fire bans, and emergency assembly points. Worked collaboratively with a university partner, with primary responsibility for front-end coding and sourcing key system components.

**Campervan Electrical System Design and Installation (2026) (Build In Progress)**
Designed a complete off-grid 12V campervan electrical system, including lithium battery storage, solar charging, DC-DC charging, power distribution, circuit protection, and battery monitoring. Applied electrical design principles, cable sizing, circuit protection, system planning, and troubleshooting to develop a practical electrical installation. Physical installation is in progress due to the wait time of  remaining components and university studies.

**Autonomous Battle Bot Project (2024) (Uni Project)**
Designed and built an autonomous battle bot using Arduino, sensors, motors, and custom electronics. Developed and integrated the power, control, electronic, and mechanical systems to create a functional autonomous robot.
